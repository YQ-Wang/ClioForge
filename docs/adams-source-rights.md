# Adams case: source rights

Checked on September 8, 2026. This is an edition-specific publication decision, not a claim that all Adams Papers content is openly licensed worldwide.

## Public case selection

Use the historical letter text from Charles Francis Adams's 1876 edition, _Familiar Letters of John Adams and His Wife Abigail Adams During the Revolution_. [Project Gutenberg ebook 34123](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/34123) explicitly identifies it as public domain in the USA. The [digital text](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/34123/34123-h/34123-h.htm) contains these letters:

| Letter                                                                 | Edition locator | Publication decision                                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Abigail Adams to John Adams, March 31, 1776, with April 5 continuation | Letter 91       | Use this edition's historical text.                               |
| John Adams to Abigail Adams, April 14, 1776                            | Letter 94       | Use this edition's historical text.                               |
| Abigail Adams to John Adams, May 7, 1776, with May 9 continuation      | Letter 102      | Use this edition's historical text; distinguish the continuation. |

Keep author, recipient, date, edition, letter number, source URL and any excerpt boundaries with each document. The digital edition reports punctuation and printer-error corrections: do not describe it as a diplomatic manuscript transcription. Preserve applicable Gutenberg redistribution notices and terms when distributing its ebook; do not imply endorsement.

The [Library of Congress's 1840 _Letters of Mrs. Adams_](https://www.loc.gov/resource/gdcmassbookdig.lettersofmrsadam04adam/?sp=1&st=grid) is another edition whose item page explicitly permits public-domain reuse. It is not automatically interchangeable with the 1876 or modern editions.

## Existing research material is not blanket-cleared

The research pilot used Founders Online transcriptions. They have **not** been replaced or revalidated against the edition above. Do not silently switch an existing source version: changed wording or pagination requires a new version and renewed citation checks.

The additional pilot letters from Abigail Adams to Mercy Otis Warren (April 27, 1776) and John Adams to James Sullivan (May 26, 1776) are excluded from this public-case selection until their exact reusable editions are documented. This exclusion is not a finding that the historical letters are copyrighted.

## Material that must remain separate

- Founders Online's [Adams project](https://founders.archives.gov/about/Adams) identifies Massachusetts Historical Society copyright. Its [document notices](https://founders.archives.gov/documents/Adams/02-01-02-0009-0001-0005) expressly cover modern annotations and other modern editorial content. Do not bundle those additions as public-domain letter text.
- NARA's [Founders Online metadata release](https://www.archives.gov/open/nhprc/dataset-founders-online) specifies attribution and noncommercial use (CC BY-NC). That statement applies to the described metadata release, not automatically to every current API or historical letter. Do not incorporate that bulk dataset into a commercial demo without separate permission or an independently verified suitable release.
- Manuscript images, modern translations and photographs require their own item-level rights checks. No such assets are cleared by this document.
- ClioForge's AGPL license covers its original code; it does not relicense third-party sources. Label the selected historical text as public domain in the USA, with edition attribution, rather than as AGPL or an invented Creative Commons license.

The public walkthrough and three attributed paragraph downloads now use this selection; see [public-site implementation](public-site.md). They are independent of the live research project and do not authorize bulk redistribution of the existing pilot corpus. No other pilot letters are distributed in this public example.
