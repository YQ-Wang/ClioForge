# Community conduct

Treat researchers, users and contributors with respect. Discuss evidence and implementation choices without personal attacks. Welcome different languages, disciplines and levels of technical experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sharing private information without consent, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Do not upload confidential archival material or other people's credentials.

Report concerns to support@canwoo.com. Maintainers may remove harmful content, limit participation or exclude contributors when needed to protect the community. Reports will be handled with care, and details shared only as needed to address the concern.
